How it works

Wish. Plan. Book in one tap.

No twenty-field form. You say what you want, TripWish builds the trip, and you decide whether to book it.

  1. 01

    Wish

    Type the trip the way you’d tell a friend. A city or a region, roughly when, who it’s for, and the kind of days you want.

  2. 02

    Plan

    TripWish turns that wish into a full trip: where to stay, how to get there, what to do, and the order of each day.

  3. 03

    Book in one tap

    Read the plan. If it’s the trip you meant, Book it all reserves it. Nothing is booked until you press that button.

  • StaysWhere you sleep, matched to the wish.
  • FlightsHow you get there and home.
  • ActivitiesThe hours you’d be sad to miss.
  • Day by dayThe trip in order, not a pile of tabs.

Stays and travel bookings via Booking.com. Booking happens in the TripWish app, which isn’t available yet — this website can’t reserve anything.

The app

From a sentence to a trip you can book

TripWish is a chatbot with a job: turn a plain-language wish into one plan, then into one booking.

  • Say it in a sentence

    A wish is the brief. “4 days in Lisbon in May, foodie, mid-budget” already has the city, the time, the mood, and the budget.

  • One plan, the whole trip

    Stays, flights, activities, and a day-by-day itinerary come back together, so you can read the trip before you commit.

  • Places you can book

    The plan is built from inventory you can reserve. Stays and travel bookings via Booking.com.

  • Book it all

    When the plan looks right, one button books the trip. You don’t reassemble it across a dozen tabs.

  • Made for iPhone

    TripWish is an iOS app, currently in development. It isn’t on the App Store yet. This site is the introduction.

  • You approve it

    The assistant proposes. You read the days, the stay, and the flights. Booking waits until you press the button.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

What is TripWish?

TripWish is an AI travel-planning chatbot for iPhone. You describe a trip in plain language, and it returns a plan you can book: stays, flights, activities, and a day-by-day itinerary.

Can I download it?

Not yet. TripWish is in development and coming soon to the App Store. This website doesn’t offer a download, and it can’t book a trip.

What should a wish look like?

A normal sentence. Where you want to go, roughly when, and what kind of trip it is. “Weekend in Kyoto, temples and ramen” and “10 days in Italy by train, two adults” are both wishes.

What does a plan include?

A place to stay, flights, activities, and a day-by-day itinerary. The Lisbon card on this page shows that shape. It is an illustration, not a live offer or a booking.

How do bookings work?

In the app, one Book it all button books the trip you just reviewed. Stays and travel bookings via Booking.com. Booking isn’t available on this website, and the app isn’t released yet.

Is TripWish part of Booking.com?

No. TripWish is an independent app in development. It is not operated or endorsed by Booking.com. Stays and travel bookings via Booking.com.

Does this website collect information?

The site is static. There’s no account and no form. If you email us, we use that message to reply. Details are in the privacy policy.
